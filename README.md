# WisdomTeleology — حکمت غایت‌شناختی

**Status:** Foundational repository — v0.1 draft baseline  
**Root position:** Fifth foundational root of Wisdom Intercessor

## Definition
WisdomTeleology studies and governs purpose, ultimate and civilizational ends, mission/vision hierarchy, long-range direction, coherence between ends and objectives, purpose realization criteria, and accountable mission-drift review.

## Five foundational roots
1. **WisdomOntology — هستی‌شناسی:** What exists; concepts, entities, relations and models.
2. **WisdomEpistemology — معرفت‌شناسی:** How claims, evidence, provenance, uncertainty and revision are handled.
3. **WisdomAxiology — ارزش‌شناسی:** What is valuable; rights, values, ethical constraints and impacts.
4. **WisdomTeleology — غایت‌شناسی:** Toward what ends; purpose, ultimate aims, mission, vision and direction.
5. **WisdomPraxeology — کنش‌شناسی/عمل‌شناسی:** How ends translate into deliberation, decisions, authorization, plans, actions and feedback.

## Scope boundaries
Teleology sets and examines purpose and long-range ends. It does not own factual validation (Epistemology), canonical concept models (Ontology), rights or ethical standards (Axiology), operational authorization or execution (Praxeology), or meta-architecture (WisdomCore). Interfaces are shared; ownership is distinct.

## Governance
Purpose is explicit, traceable, revisable through accountable processes, pluralism-aware, evidence-informed and constrained by human dignity and rights. Declared purpose does not prove feasibility, truth, or moral permissibility.

Changes are submitted through branches and Pull Requests. No merge, release or deployment occurs without owner approval.
