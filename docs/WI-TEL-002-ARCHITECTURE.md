# WI-TEL-002 — Architecture
Version 0.1 | Draft for review

## 1. Objective
Provide a purpose-and-ends capability without duplicating the other four roots, WisdomCore or operational systems.

## 2. Components
- **Purpose Registry:** controlled proposed/approved records.
- **Mission & Vision Hierarchy:** foundational purpose, mission, vision, strategic ends and subordinate objectives.
- **End Coherence Evaluator:** identifies alignment, gaps and conflicts.
- **Horizon Model:** records time horizons and dependencies; forecasts are not certainty.
- **Purpose Realization Criteria:** qualitative and quantitative evaluation criteria.
- **Mission Drift Monitor:** examines priority, incentives, decisions, activity and outcomes versus approved purpose.
- **Review & Revision:** consultation, challenge, reconsideration, approvals and version history.
- **Interface Contracts:** exchanges with roots, WisdomCore and operational layers.

## 3. Lifecycle
Draft → capture rationale/scope/assumptions/horizon/affected parties → Epistemic evidence and uncertainty review → Axiological rights/values review → Teleological coherence review → Praxeological feasibility/authority review → governance decision (approve/revise/defer/reject) → versioned record → monitoring → scheduled or triggered review. This process does not authorize action by itself.

## 4. Ownership interfaces
Ontology provides canonical models and terms. Epistemology evaluates claims, sources, evidence and uncertainty. Axiology provides rights, values, constraints and impacts. Teleology owns purpose and end coherence. Praxeology owns operational goals, options, deliberation, authorization, planning, execution and feedback. WisdomCore owns meta-architecture, integration, registry and governance controls. Operational layers retain their domain and authority.

## 5. Invariants
Stable IDs and versions; status distinguishes proposal from approval; purpose cannot grant operational authority; rights, safeguards, evidence limits and governance cannot be overridden; each end has owner, rationale, horizon and review trigger; conflicts and uncertainty are surfaced; shared concepts use interface contracts; plural purpose interpretations may be represented.

## 6. Eight-layer relationship
Teleology is a root, not a ninth operational layer. It can inform L1 WisdomNetwork, L2 WisdomChain, L3 WisdomTreasury, L4 WisdomKnowledge, L5 WisdomGuard, L6 WisdomGovernance & Justice, L7 WisdomLife & Health and L8 Wisdom of Existence & Time. Each retains domain ownership and operational controls.

## 7. Quality attributes
Auditability, interpretability, accessible and multilingual language, traceable revisions, privacy-minimizing consultation, safeguards against metric manipulation, distinction between consensus and truth, ability to record dissent.

## 8. Failure modes
Ambiguity → definition/scope/examples; drift → scheduled and triggered reviews; metric gaming → multiple indicators and qualitative evaluation; hidden assumptions → disclosure; overlap → ownership matrix/escalation; coercive purpose claims → rights review and transparency; conflicting ends → documented conflict and accountable decision.
