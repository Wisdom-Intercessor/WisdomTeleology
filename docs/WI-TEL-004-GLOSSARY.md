# WI-TEL-004 — Glossary & Root Boundaries
Version 0.1 | Draft for review

## Terms
- **Purpose (هدف/مقصود):** reason an entity or initiative exists or is undertaken.
- **End (غایت):** intended state/result; ultimate, intermediate or operational.
- **Telos (غایت بنیادین):** highest-level end or organizing direction within a declared framework; plural interpretations may exist.
- **Mission (مأموریت):** present role and contribution through which purpose is pursued.
- **Vision (چشم‌انداز):** articulated desired future state, not a forecast or guarantee.
- **Strategic end (غایت راهبردی):** longer-horizon intended state guiding strategy.
- **Objective (هدف):** bounded assessable result; operational ownership normally belongs to Praxeology or responsible system.
- **Purpose coherence (انسجام غایتی):** consistency of subordinate ends/activity with approved purpose.
- **Mission drift (انحراف مأموریت):** material divergence of priorities, incentives, decisions, activity or outcomes from approved purpose.
- **Realization criterion (معیار تحقق):** indicator or qualitative test; not the purpose itself.
- **Horizon (افق زمانی):** timeframe associated with an end or review.
- **Unintended consequence (پیامد ناخواسته):** effect not intended or adequately anticipated.
- **Purpose record:** versioned record of end, scope, rationale, assumptions, horizon, ownership, status, review and traceability.

## Root ownership
Ontology: entities, relations, models and categories.  
Epistemology: claims, evidence, provenance, uncertainty, reasoning and revision.  
Axiology: values, rights, ethical constraints, fairness and impact.  
Teleology: purpose, ultimate ends, mission, vision, direction and end coherence.  
Praxeology: operational goals, options, deliberation, decisions, authorization, plans, execution and feedback.

## Boundary rules
1. Teleology may identify relevant questions; Epistemology owns evidence evaluation.
2. Teleology may articulate rights-respecting ends; Axiology owns normative standards.
3. Teleology sets long-range direction; Praxeology owns operational translation and action lifecycle.
4. WisdomCore owns meta-architecture and cross-ecosystem governance, not every purpose statement.
5. Overlap is resolved through shared vocabulary/contracts, not duplicated root ownership.

## Names
Canonical: WisdomTeleology. Persian: غایت‌شناسی حکمت. Short form: Teleology / غایت‌شناسی. Terms remain provisional until master-registry integration.
