# WI-TEL-003 — Requirements
Version 0.1 | Draft for review

Priority: MUST mandatory; SHOULD recommended; MAY optional.

## Functional
- TEL-F-001 MUST distinguish proposed, approved, superseded and retired purpose records.
- TEL-F-002 MUST assign stable ID, version, owner, scope, rationale, status, dates and review trigger.
- TEL-F-003 MUST model foundational purpose, mission, vision, strategic ends and subordinate objectives.
- TEL-F-004 MUST record horizon, dependencies, assumptions and foreseeable unintended effects for material ends.
- TEL-F-005 MUST trace approved ends to initiatives, systems, objectives and evaluation criteria.
- TEL-F-006 MUST record alignment assessment, conflicts, gaps, uncertainty and dissent.
- TEL-F-007 MUST support scheduled and event-triggered mission-drift review.
- TEL-F-008 MUST record revision history, rationale, consultation and approval.
- TEL-F-009 MUST separate purpose approval from operational authorization and execution.
- TEL-F-010 SHOULD support accessible, multilingual summaries.
- TEL-F-011 SHOULD represent plural legitimate interpretations where consensus is not required or attainable.
- TEL-F-012 MAY support structured alternatives and scenario analysis.

## Interfaces
- TEL-I-001 MUST use Ontology canonical terms and IDs when available.
- TEL-I-002 MUST obtain Epistemology review of material factual assumptions, evidence and uncertainty.
- TEL-I-003 MUST obtain Axiology review of rights, values, ethics and affected-party impacts.
- TEL-I-004 MUST exchange ends/coherence criteria with Praxeology and receive actionability, authority and feedback assessments.
- TEL-I-005 MUST register interfaces, conflicts and dependencies with WisdomCore.
- TEL-I-006 SHOULD maintain a purpose-to-eight-layer traceability matrix.

## Governance
- TEL-G-001 MUST preserve audit trail for proposals, review, approval and revisions.
- TEL-G-002 MUST not bypass governance or create action authority.
- TEL-G-003 MUST permit challenge, reconsideration, revision and retirement.
- TEL-G-004 MUST disclose material assumptions, uncertainty, conflicts and dissent.
- TEL-G-005 MUST provide escalation for cross-root ownership disputes.
- TEL-G-006 SHOULD use independent or multi-stakeholder review for material foundational-purpose changes.

## Quality
- TEL-Q-001 MUST use stable IDs and controlled versions.
- TEL-Q-002 MUST expose record status and provenance.
- TEL-Q-003 MUST not treat metrics or forecasts as proof of purpose realization.
- TEL-Q-004 MUST minimize personal data in consultation records.
- TEL-Q-005 SHOULD be comprehensible to non-specialists.
- TEL-Q-006 SHOULD support structured/exportable records when implementation begins.

## Verification
Document inspection, registry consistency, interface/boundary review, traceability validation, governance scenario walkthroughs and sample drift assessments. Implementation tests are deferred.
